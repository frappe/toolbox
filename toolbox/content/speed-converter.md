## How it works

The converter holds every speed as **meters per second**. That is the base unit of the measurement.
A conversion takes two steps. The value you type is multiplied by the factor of the unit it is
written in, which gives meters per second, and that figure is then divided by the factor of the unit
you asked for.

The factors are fixed. One kilometer per hour is `1 / 3.6` meters per second. One mile per hour is
`0.44704` meters per second. One knot is `1852 / 3600` meters per second. One foot per second is
`0.3048` meters per second.

1. Choose the unit you are converting from.
2. Type the speed.
3. Choose the unit you want.
4. Read the answer in the other box.

Five units are offered: meter per second, kilometer per hour, mile per hour, knot and foot per
second. Both boxes accept a value. Type in the lower one and the upper one answers, so the
conversion runs in either direction without moving the units.

A result is shown to 12 significant digits. The arithmetic runs in your browser.

## Worked examples

### 100 kilometers per hour in miles per hour

The answer is **62.1371192237 mph**.

The same pair read the other way gives 60 mph as 96.56064 km/h. That second figure is exact rather
than rounded, because the international mile is defined as 1,609.344 meters.

### A wind reported at 16 knots

16 knots is **29.632 km/h**, and 18.4124711684 mph.

A knot is one nautical mile per hour and a nautical mile is 1,852 meters, so the kilometer figure is
exact as well. Only the mile figure carries a rounded tail.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many miles per hour is 100 km/h?

62.1371192237 mph. One kilometer per hour is 0.621371192237 mph, so any figure in km/h can be
multiplied by that number. 30 km/h is 18.6411357671 mph, 50 km/h is 31.0685596119 mph, and 120 km/h
is 74.5645430685 mph.

### How do I convert meters per second to kilometers per hour?

Multiply by 3.6. One meter per second is 3.6 km/h and 10 m/s is 36 km/h. The other direction divides
by 3.6, so one kilometer per hour is 0.277777777778 m/s and 100 km/h is 27.7777777778 m/s.

In miles per hour the same speed reads differently again. One meter per second is 2.23693629205 mph
and 10 m/s is 22.3693629205 mph.

### What is a knot worth in km/h and mph?

One knot is 1.852 km/h, 1.15077944802 mph and 0.514444444444 m/s. Twenty knots is 37.04 km/h. Read
back the other way, 100 km/h is 53.9956803456 knots.

### Which units can I convert here?

Meter per second, kilometer per hour, mile per hour, knot and foot per second, and any pair of them
in either direction. One foot per second is 0.3048 m/s, and one meter per second is 3.28083989501
ft/s. For distances on their own rather than speeds, use the [Length Converter](/length-converter).

### How exact is the answer?

Every unit here is defined by an exact number, so a conversion that lands on a short decimal is
exact: 60 mph is exactly 96.56064 km/h, and 20 knots is exactly 37.04 km/h. Where it does not, the
result is rounded to 12 significant digits.

### Does the converter work offline, and is anything I type stored?

Yes to the first, and no to the second. The application is kept in the browser after your first
visit, so the page opens and converts with no connection. There is no account, nothing you type
reaches a server, and no result is written down.

## Good to know

The mile per hour rests on the international yard and pound agreement of 1959, which fixed the yard
at 0.9144 meters and made the mile exactly 1,609.344 meters. Every mile per hour figure on this page
follows from that one definition.

The knot came from a rope. A log was thrown from a moving ship and the knots in its line were
counted as they ran out over a measured time.
