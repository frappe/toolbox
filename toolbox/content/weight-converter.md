## How it works

Every unit in this converter carries one number, its size in kilograms. The kilogram is the base,
and its own number is 1. A gram is 0.001. A pound is 0.45359237.

A conversion is two steps. The tool multiplies your value by the number of the unit you entered,
which gives the mass in kilograms. It then divides by the number of the unit you asked for.

`result = value * fromFactor / toFactor`

1. Type the number into the first field.
2. Choose the unit you have, and the unit you want.
3. Read the converted weight in the second field.
4. Use the swap button to turn the pair around.

Both fields accept a number. Type into the second field and the first one follows, so a conversion
runs in either direction.

Seven units are available: kilogram, gram, milligram, metric tonne, pound, ounce and stone. Each one
also answers to its symbol and common names, so `lbs` finds the pound and `kilos` finds the
kilogram.

A result is rounded to 12 significant digits. The whole conversion runs in your browser.

## Worked examples

### A body weight of 70 kilograms in pounds

70 kilograms is **154.323583529** pounds. The same weight is 11.0231131092 stone.

Read it the other way and 150 pounds is 68.0388555 kilograms. 200 pounds is 90.718474 kilograms, and
80 kilograms is 12.5978435534 stone.

### 100 grams in ounces

100 grams is **3.52739619496** ounces. One ounce is 28.349523125 grams, and one kilogram is
35.2739619496 ounces.

A pound holds exactly 16 ounces and weighs 453.59237 grams. Half a kilogram, 500 grams, is
1.10231131092 pounds.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many pounds is 1 kilogram?

2.20462262185 pounds. The reverse is exact: 1 pound is 0.45359237 kilograms. For larger weights, 5
kilograms are 11.0231131092 pounds and 100 kilograms are 220.462262185 pounds.

### How many kilograms is a stone?

6.35029318 kilograms. A stone is exactly 14 pounds and 224 ounces. A weight of 70 kilograms is
11.0231131092 stone, and 10 stone is 63.5029318 kilograms.

### Which ton does this converter use?

The metric tonne only, which is exactly 1,000 kilograms. There is no short ton and no long ton in
the unit list. Enter the weight in pounds to reach either one. A US short ton of 2,000 pounds is
907.18474 kilograms, or 0.90718474 metric tonnes. A long ton of 2,240 pounds is 1016.0469088
kilograms, or 1.0160469088 metric tonnes.

### Which ounce and which pound are these?

The avoirdupois ones, the pair used for food, freight and body weight. The pound is 0.45359237
kilograms and holds 16 ounces. The troy ounce used for precious metals is a different weight, and
this converter does not offer it. There is no carat and no grain either.

### Does it convert weight or mass?

Mass. A kilogram, a gram and a pound here all name an amount of matter, and that amount does not
change with location. Weight is the force gravity applies to that mass, so it does change. In daily
use the two words are one, and these are the numbers a scale reports.

### Is anything I type stored?

No. There is no account, and nothing you type reaches a server. The application is stored in the
browser after your first visit, so the converter works with no internet connection. The last 10
results stay in the tab and go when it closes.

## Good to know

The pound is exactly 0.45359237 kilograms. The international yard and pound agreement of 1959 fixed
that number, and this converter uses it. The ounce and the stone follow from it exactly, so pound to
kilogram is a definition rather than a measurement. Kilogram to pound is that definition read
backwards, and 2.20462262185 is a rounded view of it.

Common weight conversions:

- 1 kilogram = 2.20462262185 pounds
- 1 pound = 0.45359237 kilograms
- 100 grams = 3.52739619496 ounces
- 1 ounce = 28.349523125 grams
- 1 stone = 6.35029318 kilograms
- 1 stone = 14 pounds
- 1 metric tonne = 2204.62262185 pounds

Rounding is easy to see at the small end. Convert 1 gram to milligrams and the answer is 1000, which
is right. The arithmetic behind it lands a fraction above 1000, because a computer holds 0.001 in
binary. The rounding at 12 significant digits removes the difference.

The nine converters share one workspace, so a value typed here is still in the field when you open
another measurement. For what a container holds rather than what it weighs, use the
[Volume Converter](/volume-converter). For arithmetic on a result, use the
[Calculator](/calculator).
