## How it works

Toolbox is made by Frappe, and it is free. There is no account, no sign-up, and no payment. Nothing
you type into a tool is stored on a server, and most tools never send it anywhere at all.

It exists for a plain reason, and saying it is better than pretending otherwise. Frappe builds
software for businesses, and most people have never heard of us. A set of tools that are genuinely
useful is a way to meet a few of them. If a converter or a calculator does its job today, some
small number of the people who used it will look at what else we make. That is the whole strategy.

That gives us one rule about this site, which is worth writing down:

1. The tools come first. A tool that is worse because of a link is a bad trade.
2. Nothing is stored about you, so nothing about you is ours to sell or to lose.
3. There are no advertisements, no trackers, and no cookies you have to dismiss.
4. Every claim on the site is checkable, and the [data sources](/data-sources) page names where
   each dataset came from.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who makes Toolbox?

[Frappe](https://frappe.io), a software company. We are best known for
[ERPNext](https://frappe.io/erpnext), an open source system that businesses run their accounting,
stock, manufacturing and payroll on.

### Is it really free, and does it stay free?

Yes, and it costs almost nothing to run, which is what makes that promise safe. The tools compute
in your browser. The four datasets are files that ship with the site. Only the currency rates call
anything at all.

### Do you track me?

No. There is no analytics script, no advertising tag and no cookie banner, because there is nothing
to consent to. Your settings, your history and your saved places stay in the browser tab you are
using and go when it closes.

Two things are kept for longer, and neither says anything about you. The light or dark theme, so a
returning visitor is not shown a white flash. And the last table of currency reference rates,
which is a public list of numbers and the only thing that lets the converter work with no
connection.

### Can I see the code?

Yes. Toolbox is on [GitHub](https://github.com/frappe/toolbox) under the GNU Affero General Public
License v3. The tools, the datasets and the words on these pages are all in that repository.

### Can I use the results commercially?

The tools, yes. A dataset carries its own licence, and each one is named on the
[data sources](/data-sources) page along with the terms it came under. Two of them ask for
attribution.

### Something is wrong on a page. Where do I say so?

Open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/frappe/toolbox). A calculation that disagrees with the
tool, a definition that reads oddly, a dataset that has gone stale: all of it is worth reporting,
and the repository is the place.

## Good to know

Toolbox is built on the same things Frappe builds everything on. The
[Frappe Framework](https://frappe.io/framework) serves it and holds the datasets. The interface uses
[Frappe UI](https://ui.frappe.io), the component library our own products use. It runs on
[Frappe Cloud](https://frappe.io/cloud), which is how we host Frappe applications for other people
too.

So this site is a small, honest sample of the stack. It was built by one team in a few weeks, which
is roughly the point.

Frappe works in a way that is unusual enough to be worth reading about, whether or not you ever use
anything we make. Our [culture](https://frappe.io/culture) and our
[values](https://frappe.io/values) are written down in public, including the parts that cost us
something.
