## How it works

Every unit in this converter carries one number, its size in liters. The liter is the base, and its
own number is 1. A milliliter is 0.001. A US gallon is 3.785411784.

A conversion is two steps. The tool multiplies your value by the number of the unit you entered,
which gives the volume in liters. It then divides by the number of the unit you asked for.

`result = value * fromFactor / toFactor`

1. Type the number into the first field.
2. Choose the unit you have, and the unit you want.
3. Read the converted volume in the second field.
4. Use the swap button to turn the pair around.

Both fields accept a number. Type into the second field and the first one follows, so a conversion
runs in either direction.

Nine units are available: liter, milliliter, cubic meter, cubic centimeter, cubic inch, cubic foot,
US gallon, imperial gallon and US fluid ounce. The two gallons are separate units, because they are
different volumes.

A result is rounded to 12 significant digits. The whole conversion runs in your browser.

## Worked examples

### A 50 liter fuel tank in gallons

50 liters is **13.2086026179** US gallons. The same tank is **10.998462415** imperial gallons.

One volume, two answers. The imperial gallon is larger, so it takes fewer of them to fill the tank.
Read it the other way and 20 US gallons are 75.70823568 liters.

### A 500 milliliter bottle in US fluid ounces

500 milliliters is **16.9070113509** US fluid ounces. A full liter is 33.8140227018 US fluid ounces,
and 2 liters are 67.6280454037 US fluid ounces.

Read it the other way and a 12 US fluid ounce can is 354.88235475 milliliters, while 8 US fluid
ounces are 236.5882365 milliliters.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many liters are in a gallon?

That depends on the gallon. A US gallon is 3.785411784 liters and an imperial gallon is 4.54609
liters. Both numbers are exact. One liter is 0.264172052358 US gallons, or 0.219969248299 imperial
gallons.

### Why do the US gallon and the imperial gallon differ?

They were defined from different things and never reconciled. The US gallon is exactly 231 cubic
inches, which this converter confirms: enter 231 cubic inches and the result is 1 US gallon. The
imperial gallon is defined directly in metric units as exactly 4.54609 liters. One imperial gallon
is 1.2009499255 US gallons, so an imperial gallon holds about a fifth more.

### How many milliliters are in a US fluid ounce?

29.5735295625 milliliters, and that number is exact. One liter is 33.8140227018 US fluid ounces, and
one US gallon is exactly 128 US fluid ounces. The imperial fluid ounce is a different volume, and
this converter does not offer it.

### How large is a cubic foot?

28.316846592 liters, which is 7.48051948052 US gallons and exactly 1,728 cubic inches. A cubic meter
is 1,000 liters exactly, and 35.3146667215 cubic feet.

### Does the converter offer cups, pints or tablespoons?

No. The units are liter, milliliter, cubic meter, cubic centimeter, cubic inch, cubic foot, US
gallon, imperial gallon and US fluid ounce. A cup has no single agreed size, so a recipe is safer
converted through the fluid ounce or the milliliter.

### Is anything I type stored?

No. There is no account, and nothing you type reaches a server. The application is stored in the
browser after your first visit, so the converter works with no internet connection. No result is
written down.

## Good to know

A milliliter and a cubic centimeter are the same volume. Enter 1 cubic centimeter and the result is
1 milliliter, with no rounding. The liter is defined as one cubic decimeter, a cube 10 centimeters
on a side.

Common volume conversions:

- 1 liter = 0.264172052358 US gallons
- 1 US gallon = 3.785411784 liters
- 1 liter = 0.219969248299 imperial gallons
- 1 imperial gallon = 4.54609 liters
- 1 imperial gallon = 1.2009499255 US gallons
- 1 US fluid ounce = 29.5735295625 milliliters
- 1 liter = 33.8140227018 US fluid ounces

Check which gallon a figure uses. A car rated at 40 miles per gallon in the United Kingdom is not
the same car as one rated at 40 miles per gallon in the United States, because the gallon behind the
number is larger. The [Fuel Consumption Converter](/fuel-consumption-converter) keeps the two apart.

The nine converters share one workspace, so a value typed here is still in the field when you open
another measurement. For the sides of the container rather than what it holds, use the
[Length Converter](/length-converter).
